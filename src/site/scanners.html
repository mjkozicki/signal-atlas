SCANNERS / CAPABILITIES

Three technologies.
One curious workspace.

Each service keeps its own history. Explore demo data first, then bring your own observations.

01 / WI-FI

See what access
points announce.

Import raw 802.11 or radiotap captures. Inspect SSIDs, BSSIDs, signal levels, channels, and advertised security settings. Review findings against your authorized inventory.

Live capture: Linux, a monitor interface, iw, and dumpcap.

Wi-Fi guide ↗
02 / BLUETOOTH LOW ENERGY

Read the
advertisements.

Discover BLE devices and inspect advertised names, received signal, service identifiers, and manufacturer data. Save and compare observations without pairing.

Live discovery: Python, Bleak, a supported adapter, and OS permission.

Bluetooth guide ↗
03 / NEAR-FIELD COMMUNICATION

Bring a tag
into focus.

Present a tag to a compatible contactless reader. Inspect available identifiers and supported, readable NDEF text or URI records with read-only polling.

Live polling: Python, pyscard, and a PC/SC-compatible reader.

NFC guide ↗

02 / UNDERSTAND

More than a list
of nearby devices.

Keep the evidence and learn how to read it. The built-in About guide explains the measurements, terminology, scan process, and limits of each observation. Read the guide online.

KEEP CONTEXT

A history you can manage.

Compare snapshots, hide clutter, restore hidden entries, or delete individual snapshots. Visibility survives a restart.

TAKE IT WITH YOU

Export your observations.

Wi-Fi exports JSON, HTML, and SARIF. Bluetooth and NFC export JSON and CSV. Share only the records you choose.

KNOW THE LIMITS

Measurements, explained.

RSSI is received power, not speed or distance. Unknown fields stay unknown, and synthetic observations are labeled.
